## Important operating rule

`product-marketing` should be treated as the foundation when the workflow depends on product, audience, positioning, or market context.

Do not generate downstream campaigns from generic assumptions if the product context can be collected first.

## Quality rule

A marketing workflow is not considered successful just because content was generated.

It should be evaluated against measurable outputs such as:
- data accuracy
- lead relevance
- duplicate rate
- contact validity
- response/engagement when outreach is actually approved
- conversion or qualified-opportunity rate
- human review quality
